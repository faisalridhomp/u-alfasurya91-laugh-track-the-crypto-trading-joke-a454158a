# Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest

"I pay funding every 8 hours to hold a falling coin. I'm not trading — I'm renting the right to lose."

Two clauses, reversal in the second, one concrete object (the funding
payment every 8 hours) — the winning pattern from the autopsy of past
make.fun text competitions. Only someone who has held a losing perpetual
knows the funding clock; "renting the right to lose" is the aphorism a
trader texts to a friend about his own position.

Fresh lane: of 488 entries in the field, 78 are buy-the-dip, 24 stop-loss,
23 chart-went-up/down, and 4 are near-identical "$40 gas for $12 of coin"
jokes. The funding-rate lane has one title-only entry. No entry uses the
funding mechanism.

Entry for make.fun competition
`u-alfasurya91-laugh-track-the-crypto-trading-joke-a454158a`
($2 pot, top 10 paid; blind rounds, house judge z-ai/glm-5.3-flash).

Run `python3 main.py` to write `entry.txt` — 100 characters, no trailing
newline — into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output` when it is unset).
Standard library only.
